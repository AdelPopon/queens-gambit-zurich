Additional information

How pricing works
  • The price per lesson consists of CHF 50 per group for the teacher and a price per child.
  • The price per child becomes lower the more children take part. All children at a school pay the same price.
  • Children learn in groups of 5 to 10 at their skill level: beginners or intermediate. One skill level can have several groups, for example 15 beginners and 5 intermediate children form three groups.
  • The calculator shows the standard number of groups by number of children. Depending on the skill levels, an additional group may be needed; the final group structure is confirmed with your school.
What's included
  • Chess lessons by QGZ chess teachers
  • Organisation of groups by skill level
  • A free introductory session to assess each child's level
  • One free school chess tournament per trimester
Terms & Conditions
  • The result is a non-binding estimate based on our standard pricing model, not an offer. The final price is confirmed by QGZ Kids in a written offer.
  • The invoice is based on the confirmed number of children and the lessons actually held.
  • Lessons cancelled by the school (for example holidays or school events) are not charged. Lessons cancelled by us are rescheduled or credited. Lesson dates are agreed with your school before the start.
  • All prices exclude any applicable VAT.
  • The programme cost is calculated from the exact prices and rounded to CHF 0.05.
  • Prices for 5 to 9 children are provisional and subject to final approval.

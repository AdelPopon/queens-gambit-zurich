This calculator provides an indicative cost estimate. Final pricing will be confirmed by QGZ Kids based on your school’s specific requirements.

  • The number of groups is the standard calculation based on enrolment. The actual number may change depending on how the children are distributed across skill levels; the final group structure and price will be confirmed with your school.
  • The programme total is calculated from the exact prices and rounded to CHF 0.05. All prices exclude any applicable VAT.

How pricing works

  • Charged per teaching week

    Each child has one chess lesson per week. The total is the weekly price multiplied by the number of teaching weeks that actually take place.

  • Group fee and price per child

    The weekly price consists of CHF 50 per group for the teacher's time and a price per child that becomes lower the more children take part. All children at a school pay the same price.

  • Groups by playing level

    Children learn in groups of 5 to 10 at their skill level (beginners or intermediate), in at least two groups. Two skill levels do not always mean two groups: 15 beginners and 5 intermediate children form three groups. Each additional group needs another teacher, so the cost then rises a little more.

What's included

  • Chess lessons by QGZ chess teachers
  • Organisation of groups by playing level
  • A free introductory session with a short quiz to assess each child's level
  • One free school-wide chess tournament per trimester, open to all students

Your final invoice

The invoice is based on the confirmed number of enrolled children and the teaching weeks actually held. Lessons cancelled by the school (for example holidays or school events) are not charged; lessons cancelled by us are rescheduled or credited. The lesson dates are agreed with your school before the start.

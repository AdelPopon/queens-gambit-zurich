Who we are

Queen's Gambit Zürich is a women-oriented chess club and community. It offers a supportive way into chess: a place to start, keep playing and improve.

Our meetings

Our chess club meets regularly through the season, alternating Fridays from 19:00 and Sundays from 15:00. The winter season 2026/27 takes place at Robins Caffè in Zürich. All levels welcome.

Come along to one of our chess club meet-ups, or get in touch. We look forward to meeting you.

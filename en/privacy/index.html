Privacy Policy

How we handle data on this website.

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit this website, register for an event or contact us. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

1. Who is responsible

Queen's Gambit Zürich, a community project based in Zürich, Switzerland.
Responsible person: Adela Ioana Popon
Email: info@queensgambitzurich.ch

2. In short

This website is mainly informational. It has no user accounts and no newsletter. The only personal data we ask for is in the registration form for selected events (section 5), which you use voluntarily. The website uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not embed content from social networks. Fonts, images and scripts are delivered from this website itself.

3. Hosting and technical data

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you visit the website, your browser connects to GitHub's servers, which necessarily process technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of the request and your browser type in order to deliver the pages. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. We do not receive or analyse this data ourselves.

GitHub processes this data in the USA. GitHub participates in the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Switzerland recognises an adequate level of data protection for certified US companies. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Contacting us by email

You can contact us at info@queensgambitzurich.ch. This mailbox is provided by GoDaddy (GoDaddy Operating Company, LLC, USA). We also use queensgambitzurich@gmail.com, a Gmail account provided by Google; we may reply from this address.

When you email us, we process your email address, your name if you give it, the content of your message and any attachments, and the technical information that comes with every email, such as the date and time. We use this information only to answer your message and to deal with your request, for example a question about one of our events. We do not use it for advertising or newsletters, and we do not pass it on to anyone else.

Your messages are stored on the servers of GoDaddy and Google, which may process them in the USA and other countries. Both GoDaddy and Google participate in the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We delete emails when they are no longer needed for the purpose for which you sent them.

5. Registering for an event

For selected events, such as QGZ × WINZ, you can register on this website. We then process your full name, your email address, your participation type, your Elo rating if you choose to give it, the language of the form and the date and time of your registration.

We use this data only to manage your registration: to confirm it, to plan the event (for example the blitz tournament pairings) and to email you about this event. We do not use it for advertising or newsletters, and we do not pass it on to anyone else. Only the QGZ organisers have access.

The form sends your details to a Google Apps Script service in our Google account. Your registration is stored in a private Google Sheets spreadsheet, and the confirmation emails are sent via Google. When you submit the form, your browser connects to Google's servers, which also process your IP address. Google LLC may process this data in the USA and participates in the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. To protect the form against spam, we use a hidden check field and a timing check; no cookies or tracking are involved.

You register by ticking the consent box. You can withdraw your consent and cancel your registration at any time by emailing info@queensgambitzurich.ch; we then delete your registration. Otherwise we delete registrations no later than three months after the event.

6. Links to other websites

Our website links to our Instagram profile, partner websites such as WINZ and other external websites. No data is sent to these services when you visit our website; it is only when you follow a link that you leave our website, and the privacy policy of that provider applies, for Instagram that of Meta Platforms.

7. Your rights

Under the FADP you can ask us for information about personal data we process about you, and you can request its correction or deletion. Please write to info@queensgambitzurich.ch. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) at edoeb.admin.ch.

8. Changes

We will update this privacy policy if the website or the way we process data changes. The current version is always published on this page.

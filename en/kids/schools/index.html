Additional information

How pricing works
  • The price per lesson consists of CHF 50 per group for the teacher and a price per child.
  • The price per child becomes lower the more children take part. All children at a school pay the same price.
  • Children learn in groups of 5 to 10 at their skill level: beginners or intermediate. One skill level can have several groups, for example 15 beginners and 5 intermediate children form three groups.
  • The calculator shows the standard number of groups by number of children. Depending on the skill levels, an additional group may be needed; the final group structure is confirmed with your school.
What's included
  • Free trial day: a complimentary introductory chess session, so your school can experience our teaching approach before committing to a programme.
  • Skill assessment: a short chess quiz to evaluate each child's playing level and organise suitable learning groups.
  • School chess tournament: one complimentary tournament per trimester, open to all students, to encourage participation, celebrate progress and make chess enjoyable.
  • Original learning materials: our own educational materials, for use in lessons and at home.

Regular chess lessons by QGZ teachers are of course included.

Terms & Conditions
  • The calculator provides a non-binding estimate. Final pricing is confirmed through a written offer.
  • Invoicing is based on confirmed enrolment and lessons actually delivered.
  • Lessons cancelled by the school are not charged. Lessons cancelled by QGZ Kids are rescheduled or credited.
  • Prices exclude any applicable VAT.

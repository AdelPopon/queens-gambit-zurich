Community

Our Community

Queen's Gambit Zürich is a women-oriented chess club and community. It offers a supportive way into chess: a place to start, keep playing and improve.

  • Regular chess meetings

    Active

    Our chess club meets regularly through the season, alternating Fridays from 19:00 and Sundays from 15:00. The winter season 2026/27 takes place at Robins Caffè in Zürich. All levels welcome.

  • Coaching Sessions

    Improve your chess with guided sessions led by our coach, Sinan Deveci. Open to all skill levels.

  • Social chess

    Relaxed games, conversation and the chance to meet new people. You don't need to know anyone to join.

  • An inclusive community

    QGZ is primarily designed for women. We also welcome transgender and non-binary members, and everyone who shares our values and mission.

We see QGZ as a bridge into the wider chess world, and we encourage members to take part in tournaments, clubs and chess activities beyond QGZ.

    Kids Chess

    Chess for the Next Generation

    Beyond Champions. Inspiring a Lifelong Love for Chess.

    We believe every child deserves the opportunity to discover their potential through chess. Some may become champions, others may simply enjoy playing for years to come. Both are success stories.

    Our approach combines learning, encouragement and individual development, keeping curiosity and enthusiasm at the heart of the experience.

    • 01

      Passion First

      We foster curiosity, enjoyment and enthusiasm in both students and educators.

    • 02

      Every Child Can Grow

      We support individual abilities, learning speeds and ambitions, including advanced development for exceptionally talented children.

    • 03

      A Lifelong Connection to Chess

      We encourage lasting participation, regardless of competitive achievements.

    Every Journey Matters.

    “Whether a child dreams of becoming a champion or simply loves playing chess, we believe every journey deserves encouragement, recognition and the opportunity to grow.”

    About

    Who We Are

    Queen's Gambit Zürich exists to help women enter, grow and fully integrate into the chess community.

    Our role is not to replace the existing chess community. Our role is to help more women become part of it. We aim to become the leading reference point for women interested in chess in Zürich.

    What guides us

    • Competence
    • Commitment
    • Collaboration
    • Mutual respect

    QGZ is primarily designed for women and welcomes everyone who shares these values.

Deutsch

Vom ersten Zug bis zum ersten Turnier!

Queen's Gambit Zürich lädt Kinder zu einem besonderen Schach-Workshop in Zollikon ein: drei Vormittage für Anfängerinnen und Anfänger und für junge Spielerinnen und Spieler mit Erfahrung.

Sa 24.Lernen & Entdecken09:30–11:30

English

From the first move to the first tournament!

Queen's Gambit Zürich invites young chess enthusiasts to a special workshop in Zollikon: three mornings for beginners and for young players with experience.

Sat 24Learn & Discover09:30–11:30
Sun 25Practice & Challenge09:30–11:30
Sat 31Tournament & Awards09:00–12:00

Combi Face, Oberdorfstrasse 37, Zollikon · ages 5–13 · max. 18 children

Beginners: from the first move to the first checkmate, through games. Intermediate: sharpen tactics and play a friendly, unrated tournament.

Included
Learning materials, a personal Chess Passport, healthy snacks, the final tournament and a certificate. Taught in German, with English when needed.

Places are limited. Parents are welcome from 11:30 on 31 October for the award ceremony.
